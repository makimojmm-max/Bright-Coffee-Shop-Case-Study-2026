KEY INSIGHTS (ALL DATA)

  • Revenue doubled from $81.7k in January to $166.5k in June (+104%), driven by more transactions; the average ticket stayed about $4.70.
  • 08:00-10:59 brings in 36.7% of revenue. After 11:00 hourly revenue halves and stays flat until 18:00, the biggest growth opportunity.
  • Coffee (38.6%) and Tea (28.1%) earn two-thirds of revenue; Barista Espresso is the top product type at $91.4k.
  • Stores are within 2.8% of each other on revenue, but Astoria earns 21% in the evening against 8% at Lower Manhattan.
  • 58.5% of transactions are a single item, so there is room to grow basket size with bundles and upsizing.
  • Branded, Loose Tea, Flavours and Packaged Chocolate together earn only 5.4% of revenue.
Source: Bright Coffee Shop Sales raw data (149,116 rows), cleaned as in the project SQL file: unit_price decimal comma fixed, total_amount = transaction_qty × unit_price. Revenue per day uses calendar days in the selected months (every day had sales). Built for the Bright Coffee Shop case study.
